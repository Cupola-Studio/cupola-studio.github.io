Cupola Earth Free

Named after the observation window on the International Space Station, Cupola Earth shows Earth's horizon as an astronaut would see it.

  • Daylight, sunset and city lights move across the planet with the real time of day, adjusted for daylight saving.
  • The sun travels above the horizon during the day; the moon crosses the sky every hour in its real phase.
  • A live space fact on the edge: distance travelled around the Sun today, distance to the Sun, or how old the sunlight reaching you is.
  • Steps, heart rate, sunrise or sunset, and weather, plus a battery reading. Four slots can be changed to other data.
  • Five accent colours and a dark always-on mode that lights very few pixels.

Requires Wear OS 5 or later, including Samsung Galaxy Watch.

How the numbers are worked out

Everything is calculated on your watch from the date and time, with no internet connection and no location.

  • Distance to the Sun comes from Earth's orbit (about 147.1 million km in early January, 152.1 million km in early July).
  • Sunlight age is that distance divided by the speed of light, about 8 minutes 10 to 27 seconds through the year.
  • Travelled today is Earth's orbital speed (29.3 to 30.3 km/s) times the time since midnight.
  • The light on the planet follows solar time, so it is close to, but not exactly, your local sunrise and sunset, which depend on where you live.

Distances use a simplified orbit model accurate to well under 1%.

Common questions

A slot shows a word instead of a number. Some data, such as Samsung's stress reading, is only shared with Samsung's own watch faces. Steps, heart rate, UV, battery and weather work.

The sunset on the face is not exactly my local sunset. The face does not use your location, so it follows solar time for your time zone.

Privacy policy

Cupola watch faces collect no personal data.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • The faces request no permissions and do not connect to the internet.
  • Health, weather and other readings shown in slots come from apps already on your watch. They are displayed on the watch only; Cupola does not store or send them anywhere.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
